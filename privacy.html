Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

Account info you provide directly: username, email, optional full name and date of birth. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from Google. Daily snapshot data you log: mood, energy, sleep, screen time, income/expenses/savings, goals, decisions, and similar self-reported metrics. This data is self-reported personal reflection, not medical or financial records.

2. How We Use Your Data

To calculate your VEKTRA score, generate reports, and provide the service you sign up for. To generate AI narrative reports, relevant portions of your logged data (not your name or contact details) are sent to Anthropic's Claude API for processing under Anthropic's own data handling terms.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on our hosted database infrastructure and transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. We use standard security practices, but no system is 100% secure.

4. Third Parties We Use

Anthropic (AI report generation), Stripe and Paystack (payment processing — we never see or store your full card/M-Pesa details), Google (Sign-In authentication and web fonts), Cloudflare (content delivery for a library used to generate shareable images), and our hosting providers. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers or data brokers.

5. Your Rights

You can export your logged data as CSV/JSON at any time from within the app. To request correction or deletion of your account and data, email vektraapp@gmail.com — we will action verified requests within 30 days. (Self-service in-app account deletion is planned but not yet available.)

6. Cookies and Local Storage

See our Cookie Policy for full detail. In short: we use browser local storage (not cookies) for login sessions and preferences, and do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Children's Privacy

VEKTRA is not intended for children under 13, and accounts/subscriptions require you to be 18 or the age of majority in your jurisdiction (see Terms). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. International Data Transfers

Our service providers (hosting, AI, payments) operate infrastructure in multiple countries, so your data may be processed outside your home country.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or data requests: vektraapp@gmail.com.
