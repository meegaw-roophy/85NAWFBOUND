Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. We Don't Use Tracking Cookies

VEKTRA does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or any third-party tracking cookies. We don't run Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar tools.

2. What We Actually Use: Local Storage

Instead of cookies, the VEKTRA app uses your browser's local storage to keep you logged in, remember your preferences (like theme), and save unsaved drafts. This data stays on your device, is not readable by other websites, and is considered "strictly necessary" for the app to function — it doesn't require consent under most privacy laws.

3. Cookies Set by Third-Party Services

A few features load scripts from other companies, and those companies may set their own cookies under their own privacy policies, independent of VEKTRA:

→ Google Sign-In — when you choose to sign in with Google.

→ Google Fonts — used to load our typeface.

→ Cloudflare (cdnjs) — used only when you generate a shareable report image.

4. Our Own Analytics

Our marketing landing page logs anonymous click events (which button was clicked, not who clicked it) directly to our own server — no cookie is set to do this.
