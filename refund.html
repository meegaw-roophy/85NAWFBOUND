Refund Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. No Refunds

All subscription purchases are final and non-refundable. Once a subscription is purchased, you will not be eligible for a refund of any unused portion.

2. Prorated Upgrades

When upgrading between tiers, the remaining value of your current subscription may be prorated and applied to the new tier at our discretion.

3. Service Interruptions

In the unlikely event of extended service outages (exceeding 48 hours), affected users may be eligible for account credit extensions at our sole discretion.

4. Payment Processing Errors

If you are charged in error due to a payment processing failure, please contact us within 30 days for resolution and potential refund.

5. Cancellation Policy

You can cancel your subscription at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. No refunds are provided for the remaining period.

6. Exceptions

Refund requests will only be considered in exceptional circumstances at our sole discretion. Each case is reviewed individually.

7. Contact

For refund-related inquiries, contact us at vektraapp@gmail.com with your transaction details.
